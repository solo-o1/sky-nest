About Us

A home base for people building a life on the move.

Who we are

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces provides flexible short-term and long-term co-living accommodation in Singapore. Demand for our rooms has grown quickly as more people work remotely or relocate for work and study, and every SkyNest building is designed around that shift.

Our residents get a fully furnished private room, shared spaces for working and socialising, and a community that understands what it is like to arrive in a new city.

Who SkyNest is for

Young professionals

Relocating for a new role or project and need somewhere ready to live in.

Digital nomads

Need reliable Wi-Fi and a proper desk, not just a hotel room.

International students

Want a safe, furnished room and a community while they study.

Companies & groups

Need custom, long-term or accessible accommodation for their people.

What makes us different

Flexible stays

From one week to long-term, with no traditional lease commitment.

Move-in ready

Every room is fully furnished, with utilities included in every package.

Built for work

Co-working space and high-speed Wi-Fi are part of the building, not an afterthought.

Real community

Weekly events make it easy to meet people in a new city.
