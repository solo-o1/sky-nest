About SkyNest

Built for people who relocate, not just visit

Remote work and global study have changed how people need to live. SkyNest was designed around that shift: flexible stays, real community, and none of the friction of a traditional long-term lease.

Who SkyNest is for

  • Young professionals relocating for a new role or project
  • Digital nomads who need reliable Wi-Fi and a desk, not a hotel room
  • International students who want a safe, furnished space near campus
  • Corporate teams and clients needing custom or accessible accommodation

What makes it different

  • Stays from one week to long-term, with no traditional lease commitment
  • Every room is fully furnished and move-in ready
  • Co-working space and high-speed Wi-Fi are built into the building, not an afterthought
  • A social calendar that makes it easy to meet people in a new city

Facilities

Everything you need, under one roof

Use the tabs below to explore what's included at every SkyNest property.

  • Fully furnished private rooms — move in with a suitcase, not a moving van.
  • Communal kitchens and dining areas — cook your own meals or share one with the floor.
  • Laundry rooms — on every residential floor, no coins required.
  • 24/7 security and key-card access — every entry point and lift is key-card controlled, staffed around the clock.
  • Shared co-working spaces — quiet zones and collaborative tables on every floor.
  • High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the building — including every bedroom, lounge, and rooftop space.
  • Study pods — enclosed, bookable spaces for calls, exams, and deep-focus work.
  • Rooftop garden — open-air space for residents to relax or host small gatherings.
  • Gym and yoga studio — on-site, no separate membership needed.
  • Social lounge — the main gathering space for residents between work and home.
  • Weekly community events — from welcome dinners to skill-swap evenings.
